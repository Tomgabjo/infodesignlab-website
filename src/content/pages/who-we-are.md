InfoDesignLab is a socially conscious information design firm based in Oslo. Our vision was to establish a multidisciplinary lab drawing on the experience of an international team of senior professionals in the fields of information design, graphic design, cognitive science, UX/UI design and technical implementation.

We have spent the last 15 years providing bespoke information design support to scientists, making scientific knowledge effective and accessible to wider audiences. We collaborate with scientists and activists, with the public and private sector, with universities and the media. Most of the time the goal is to **co-design visualisations** that empower specialists and non-specialists to make informed decisions about the big challenges we face as a society: from education to health, from social justice to climate change.

**Angela Morelli** is an award-winning Italian information designer and the CEO and co-founder of InfoDesignLab. From climate change to health, she has worked with a wide range of scientific organisations including the IPCC, the European Environment Agency, the Norwegian Institute of Public Health, CICERO and the WMO. She gained her MA in Information Design from Central Saint Martins in London. She is an acclaimed international speaker and was named a 2012 Young Global Leader by the World Economic Forum.

**Tom Gabriel Johansen** is an award-winning Norwegian information designer and co-founder of InfoDesignLab. He has over 20 years of experience in design, from strategic design to board games, interaction design and data visualisation. He has worked with the IPCC, the EEA, NIPH, CICERO and the WMO. For seven years he worked at the Norwegian Broadcasting Corporation (NRK), embedding design and information visualisation into the news-floor operation. He has served as a special advisor for Statistics Norway and as a member of Eurostat's Visualization Task Force.

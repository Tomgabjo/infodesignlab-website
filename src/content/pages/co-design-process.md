Whether co-designing data visualisations to convey scientific findings, transforming scientific reports into engaging experiences, or conducting hands-on workshops, we see Design as a strategic problem-solving process. Design enhances our understanding by allowing us to map out interconnections, facilitate dialogues among scientists, build bridges between data experts and the audience, and involve key stakeholders.

Data visualisations are more than just tools; they enhance understanding throughout the research process and are crucial when it comes time to communicate findings. Our extensive experience working closely with the science community has shown us that co-design and user involvement is the only route to collective success when turning complex scientific data into effective visual tools for decision-making.

Co-design is centered on the involvement of all those who affect and are affected by the consequences of design. Over nearly two decades of projects, we have come to realise that it is not just **what** we design, but **how** we design it that can leave a lasting mark — both on the audience's experience and on the organisations we collaborate with. Together, we explore and stress-test solutions, achieving a sense of joint ownership of the final product.

The legacy of such a way of working does not consist only in design deliverables and effective visualisations, but also in cultural changes and mind shifts within the organisations we collaborate with.

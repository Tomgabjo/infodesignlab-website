We are lecturers and international speakers and we run hands-on workshops for businesses and organisations working in environments that require presenting information in effective and creative ways. Talks serve as a means to inspire and share our co-design process through case studies, while workshops provide an immersive, hands-on experience to learn about the design tools we use in our projects.

In 2009 we established the first short course in Information Design and Data Visualisation at Central Saint Martins University in London and taught it for a decade until Brexit and Covid hit. Since then we have continued to teach at institutions and organisations worldwide.

The courses we offer can last anywhere from half a day to several weeks, and can be tailored to the specific needs of the audience. There is a strong focus on how to identify the message we need to communicate in relation to an audience, how to transform the data into powerful stories, and how to choose the most appropriate tools in order to present those stories.

## What was done

The objective of this project was to provide our client with a hands-on learning experience in the co-design process, while at the same time developing an effective approach for communicating environmental targets.

> Through this project, we aimed to create a collaborative environment where our client could engage with the co-design process, see its power and understand its value.

Together with the communication team at Norwegian Environment Agency we initiated a pilot brief to apply our co-design process to the challenge of presenting complex environmental data to general audiences.

The collaboration between the design team, scientists, editors, a photographer and a filmmaker resulted in a series of outputs: a booklet, an online platform, and a set of short films.

## How it was done

To initiate the co-design process, we conducted in-depth conversations with our target audience to determine their knowledge and interest in the topic.

The feedback we received was particularly insightful. While many interviewees were not surprised to learn that Norway had environmental targets, most did not know what these targets were.

In-depth conversations and user adoptions are ways to involve users in the co-design process from the very beginning, where you can test whether a concept resonates or not.

## What was done

How can we visualise science in clear, powerful and engaging ways to meet the needs of our audiences? How can we foster an inclusive design process to ensure authentic participation? How can we transform data into effective tools for decision-making?

These questions drive a two-day intensive masterclass co-developed with Graphic Hunters in Utrecht. The course is aimed at scientists and researchers, as well as designers working in contexts where science must be presented effectively — capturing and mobilising the imagination of an audience across policy, communication and advocacy.

Participants are introduced to the intersection of text and image, perception and cognition, process and leadership, beauty and function, logic and emotion: the qualities that enable us to reveal the wealth of information within data and to deliver effective messages.

In a world where polarisation and misinformation rank among the top risks for the coming years, critical thinking and evidence-based decision-making are crucial in tackling challenges such as disease, social inequality, and climate change. Data visualisation plays a key role in enabling researchers and scientists to communicate complex information and support informed decisions.

## How it was done

The course unfolds through four modules, incorporating lectures, case studies for inspiration, hands-on tasks, and discussions for reflecting and learning from one another. Each module offers concrete tools and techniques designed for immediate application in participants' daily work.

**Module 1 — Process:** Co-design as a domain of design research and practice, ensuring a deep and shared understanding between those creating the visuals and the audience. Participants explore co-design methods for visualisation projects aimed at including multiple stakeholders and perspectives.

**Module 2 — Toolkit:** An introduction to the essential components of a toolkit refined over 18 years of working with scientific organisations, focusing on methods for transforming scientific findings into clear and accessible narrative.

**Module 3 — Narrative:** The dynamic between evidence and storytelling — learning to craft compelling narratives that resonate with audience needs while balancing rigour and creativity.

**Module 4 — Prototype:** Participants put into action the knowledge from previous modules to prototype a visual narrative tailored to their audience's needs.

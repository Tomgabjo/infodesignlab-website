## What was done

This report, published in 2023, is the first comprehensive global assessment of the current state of Carbon Dioxide Removal (CDR). Our task was to design the figures and the visual identity for the full report.

Scientific reports often treat communication as an afterthought, with a focus on separate outputs. However, for this project, communication was integrated from the start.

The work was planned to ensure a deep collaboration between communicators and content experts to co-develop how scientific findings are presented, ensuring clarity without sacrificing rigour.

The team at Exalt Editing has provided project coordination, story-building and editing for the entire report and appointed InfoDesignLab as the design partner.
